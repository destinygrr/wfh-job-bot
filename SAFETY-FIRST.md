# 🛡️ SAFETY FIRST — how to never get scammed by a "work from home" job in India

Read this once. It takes 5 minutes and it will save you money, documents and peace of mind.
The bot already blocks and flags most of what's below — but a human check takes 60 seconds and is final.

---

## 1. The Golden Rule (this one rule kills 99% of scams)

> **A real employer NEVER takes money from you.**
> Not a registration fee, not a security deposit, not a training fee, not a "refundable" deposit, not a laptop/kit fee, not a GST charge, not a courier charge, not an "ID card" fee.

If money must go **out of your pocket**, it is a scam — no matter how professional the website, email or office looks. Stop the conversation, and report the number.

---

## 2. Red flags — if you see two or more, walk away

| 🚩 Red flag | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Selection **without a real interview** (just a WhatsApp call or nothing at all) | Real hiring has a screening process |
| **Everything happens on personal WhatsApp/Telegram** — no company email ID | Real HR writes from `@companyname.com` |
| "**Pay ₹1,999 for the training kit / ID / software**, salary starts next week" | Classic advance-fee fraud |
| **Task-based earning apps**: "like videos, complete tasks, earn daily" and "upgrade your level to unlock bigger tasks" | India's biggest current scam (₹ lost in crores, 2023-2025). Never deposit, ever. |
| **Daily payout** promised for typing / form filling / ad posting | Typing-job fraud, 20+ years old and still running |
| Asks for **Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, cancelled cheque, OTP** before any signed offer letter | Identity theft + loan fraud in your name |
| Asks you to install **AnyDesk / TeamViewer / RustDesk** to "complete KYC" | Remote access = they empty your bank account |
| **Cheque/DD "received in error"** — please return the money | Fake-cheque scam |
| Salary that is **absurdly high for the work** ("₹60,000/month for 2 hours of data entry") | Greed is their bait |
| Company name **very close to a famous one** (`Amazoon`, `InfosysTechno Solutions`, `TCS-Hiring-Division`) | Brand-name mimicry |
| **Gmail/Rediff/Yahoo** email for an HR/"Recruitment Officer" | Real HR uses a company domain |
| Pressurising you: "**offer expires in 1 hour**", "only 2 seats left" | Manufactured urgency |
| Asks you to **recruit 2 people** to unlock your salary | MLM / pyramid |

## 3. The 60-second verification routine (do this for every job you like)

1. **Google the exact company name + the words** `scam`, `fraud`, `review`, `salary`, `complaints`.
2. **Check the company is registered** on the MCA portal → search by name and look for a **CIN**, a registered address and a real director list: https://www.mca.gov.in (free, no login for the name search).
3. **Check GST** if they claim to be an Indian business — GSTIN on the invoice must match the company name: https://www.gst.gov.in
4. **Open their LinkedIn company page** — do the *employees* look real (photos, history, mutual connections)? A 2-employee "company" hiring 300 people from home is a fantasy.
5. **Match the email domain**: an offer from `hr@companyname.com` is checkable. `companyname.hr@gmail.com` is not a company.
6. **Find the company's official phone number on Google** (not the one the recruiter gave you) and call it to confirm that the person who contacted you works there. This single step catches recruitment fraud.
7. **Ask for the written offer letter** on letterhead: company name, address, CIN, your name, role, **fixed salary**, joining date, HR contact. Then ask: *"Is any payment required from me at any stage?"* A real employer happily says no, in writing.

---

## 4. Documents — the correct order of sharing

1. **Never** before an offer: Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, photos of cheques, OTPs, UPI PIN.
2. After a **signed offer letter** and step 6 above: PAN + Aadhaar (masked, e.g. last 4 digits) + bank details is normal for payroll.
3. Never share **OTP, CVV, PIN, net-banking passwords** with *anyone* — no genuine HR ever asks.
4. Never do **video-KYC on a link sent by the recruiter** — always open the official app/site yourself.
5. Keep **screenshots** of the job post, chats and emails. If something goes wrong they become evidence.

---

## 5. If you are scammed (or suspect it)

* **Money lost** → report on the cyber-crime portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in or call **1930** (national cyber-crime helpline). Report fast — the first 24 hours matter.
* Report the job post on the platform (LinkedIn "Report job", Apna / Naukri support), so others are protected.
* Report the WhatsApp/Telegram account inside the app.
* Never pay a "recovery agent" who promises to get your money back — that is a second scam.

---

## 6. What a *genuine* WFH job looks like

* A real job description with a defined role, tools, timings, reporting manager and **fixed monthly salary** in writing.
* Interviews with 1-3 people from the company, on company email/video link.
* A **offer letter / appointment letter** on company letterhead, and an HR contact reachable on the company's official number.
* No money asked at any stage, before or after joining.
* For gig work (AI-training, tutoring): a signed contract, a clear per-task or per-hour rate **in USD or INR**, a payment schedule, and a platform you can find on the open web with real user reviews (e.g. Appen, TELUS International, Outlier/Scale, Chegg, Vedantu).
